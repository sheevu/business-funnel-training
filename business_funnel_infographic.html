Lead Segmentation

Effective lead generation distinguishes between casual interest and immediate solutions. This breakdown shows the quality mix of incoming leads.

Insight: "Hot Leads" represent the highest conversion potential and require response times under 5 minutes for maximum efficiency.

Stage Conversion Rates

Analyzing where friction occurs in the funnel. The drop-off at the 'Follow-Up' stage often highlights gaps in proposal clarity or sentiment understanding.

Insight: A 5% increase in Follow-Up conversion often leads to a 20% increase in final Close Sales due to funnel momentum.

The Follow-Up phase is the most complex psychological point in the journey. It requires a blend of **Relationship Management**, **Price Proposals**, and **Sentiment Analysis**.
